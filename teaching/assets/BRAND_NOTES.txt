IMT MINES ALES BRAND ASSETS AND IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
=================================================

Source: the user-supplied Charte graphique IMT Mines Ales, October 2024,
file imt_charte_imtales_oct(2).pdf. Page references below are the printed
page numbers in the charter, not PDF spread indices.

OFFICIAL ARTWORK

imt_mines_ales_block.{pdf,svg,png}: the official horizontal school logo
and French state brand (Marianne), clipped from the charter's first cover.
imt_mines_ales_logo.{pdf,svg,png}: the official school logo from the same
cover, without the separate Marianne block, for interior pages.

The original vector paths, proportions and colours are preserved. No logo
element was redrawn. Each asset includes a white background and H/2 clear
space on every outer side, H being the original tangram height (p.7).
The PDF and SVG are vector assets; the PNG is a faithful raster rendering.
See brand_asset_provenance.json for the source checksum, clipping bounds
and exact dimensions.

Full block: 248.630 x 105.472 pt; PNG 1800 x 764 pixels.
Standalone: 168.367 x 105.472 pt; PNG 1400 x 878 pixels.

The minimum 20 mm school-logo width specified on p.7 refers to the actual
logo, not the surrounding white canvas. Including this asset's padding:
  * full block: use at least 38.0 mm outer width; 52 mm is recommended;
  * standalone: use at least 25.72 mm outer width; 30 mm is recommended.
Scale proportionally. Do not crop the built-in clear space or independently
reposition or recolour pieces of the official artwork.

PLACEMENT

Use the full block on the first cover/opening, on white, preferably at the
upper left (pp.8-9, 24-25). The school-only mark may be used inside (p.24).
The charter defines a protected white region for the cover block using
seven grid squares horizontally and four vertically (pp.18-19), with
flexibility for landscape and special formats. The left placement and
protection zone should remain visible even on a restrained academic cover.

There is a separate website exception: the state brand precedes the school
mark at the left of a permanent website header (p.24). The supplied block
is the charter's document/report configuration, with Marianne on the right;
it is appropriate for the self-contained downloadable course report.

TYPOGRAPHY

Helvetica is the primary typeface; Archer is an optional companion only in
Light or Bold. Arial is the office fallback when the other fonts cannot be
used (pp.12-13). Titles use bold sans serif, aligned left or right; body text
is regular/light, left-aligned or justified (p.15). The report/PowerPoint
office examples are on pp.49-50.

This build environment has Nimbus Sans and Liberation Sans, but no complete
genuine Helvetica or Arial installation. Embedded fonts in the supplied PDF
are incomplete subsets: their character maps list more glyphs than have
actual outlines. They must not be used for arbitrary document text.
LaTeX may use Nimbus Sans through helvet as a metric-compatible Helvetica
surrogate. Do not describe the compiled result as using genuine Helvetica.
For HTML, prefer: Helvetica, Arial, "Nimbus Sans", "Liberation Sans",
sans-serif. Retain mathematical fonts where needed for legible formulae.

RESTRAINED ACADEMIC APPLICATION

Keep the main reading area white, body text dark, headings bold sans serif,
and use cyan for the primary identity cue with pink as a supporting accent.
Use a small number of 45-degree or 90-degree triangles on a cover (pp.20-21),
preserving the protected white logo area. In the interior, the charter asks
for a cyan triangle at least once per double page, paired with school pink
(p.40). A consistent cyan corner and a restrained pink accent can carry the
identity without obstructing equations, tables, or diagrams. The PowerPoint
example uses white pages, corner triangles, an interior footer rule, a small
school mark and page numbering (p.49).

The cover grid uses a margin equal to one tenth of the short page dimension.
The interior grid may reduce this to one twentieth; the selected interior
grid must remain consistent across pages (pp.16-17).

REFERENCE IMPLEMENTATIONS USED FOR THIS REVISION

The BIMER company-report model (template_imt_mines_ales_bimer.pdf) supplies
the document composition: a full cyan diagonal cover with navy/pink upper
triangles and a white logo field at the lower left; bold sans-serif titles
paired with a serif display face; cyan subheads; and alternating
plain/triangular folios. At the instructor's request, the student statement
uses one reading column throughout with 19-mm side margins. The instructor
guide retains the model's two-column prose. Mathematical expressions, code
and tables keep enough width to remain readable. The measured reference
geometry and original font information are in bimer_reference/.

The model's embedded Archer and Helvetica fonts are incomplete subsets and
are not reused for new text. Compiled article PDFs use Nimbus Sans and Latin
Modern Roman for the sans/serif contrast. With XeLaTeX or LuaLaTeX the shared
IMTDisplay command uses Archer Light when installed. This is an explicit
font substitution, not a claim to reproduce the proprietary typefaces.

SLIDE TEMPLATE

The slides now use the attached Graphical Models and Bayesian Networks
lecture template directly. All files in customIMTbeamer/ are preserved
byte-for-byte from that attachment. Its imt_pres class supplies the title
page, headline, footer, native page numbering, colours, font settings,
blocks and table style. No custom replacements of those templates are
defined in slides.tex. Graph-specific TikZ notation is used only to draw
the routing example. The template's Euler/EulerVM math font dependencies
are retained. The BIMER cover is used for the written documents only.

The BRAFITEC visual model is the latest
UFC_SyCoIA_EuroMov_Interactive_Research_Map.html. The STTRPSD report adopts its
institutional header, wide grid, large title, cyan/pink geometric accents,
navy-selected tabs, pale filter panels, bordered cards and dark table heads.
Only the visual structure is reused; research-map data and the source
company-report content are not distributed in the course package.
